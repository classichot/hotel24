Six Latin faces for HOTEL24

Archivo is the Modernist system's own face, so 3a is the baseline you'd be replacing — the rest are shown against it. Thai stays IBM Plex Sans Thai behind every option. What actually decides this is the wordmark, and the figures: this product is a wall of ฿ amounts, occupancy percentages and rate grids, so a face without tabular numerals costs you column alignment on every table in the console.

Note the design system is binding on style, not on this specimen: swapping the Latin face is a real deviation from Modernist. Worth doing only if the wordmark or the numerals gain something Archivo doesn't give you.

What I would pick

Stay on 3a Archivo. It is the design system's face, it has the widest weight range of the six, its 800 holds the wordmark better than anything here, and the 24 is unmistakable at nav size. Changing it costs you the system and buys very little.

If you do want to move, 3b IBM Plex Sans is the only argument I find honest: it is the Latin half of the Thai face you already chose, drawn by the same team on the same skeleton, so Thai and Latin stop being a pairing and become one typeface. You lose Archivo's 800 — Plex stops at 700, so the wordmark gets lighter — and the whole system's Latin identity shifts with it. That is the trade.
